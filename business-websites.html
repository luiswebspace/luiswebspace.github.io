A structure built around your customers

We start with what people need to know: what you offer, who it’s for, where you work, and how to contact you. That may fit on one thoughtful page or need several pages with more detail.

Pages that can make sense

A home page introduces you. A service page explains the work. An about page gives your business a human voice. A contact page makes the next step clear. We choose the pages together rather than filling a site with sections you don’t need.

Your ideas, brought into the design

Bring your colors, photos, logo, references, or rough sketch. We’ll discuss typography, layout, and the feeling you want the site to have. You review a preview before it’s published.

Features are part of the conversation

Inquiry forms, booking links, maps, galleries, and other features can be discussed. A shop or custom booking system involves different requirements from a simple informational site. Any external services and costs are explained in the quote.

Your handy checklist

  • Tell me about your customers and services
  • Share your branding, photos, and any references
  • Pick the action you want visitors to take
Good things to ask

A few questions, answered.

Can we start with one page?

Yes, if the content and features fit. We can discuss future pages as a separate phase.

Can you replace my existing website?

We can review it and discuss what to keep, what to change, and how your current domain and services are set up.

A real person, a little guidance

Still figuring it out? That’s okay.

I’m Luis. If you need help finding a domain or understanding something website-related, just mention it in your email. Advice is free; purchases and project work are discussed separately.
