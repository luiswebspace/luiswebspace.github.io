Before we publish

You approve the final website and complete the payment requirements in your agreement. We check navigation, important links, contact details, readable layouts, and the features included in the project.

Testing contact forms

We’ll test how an inquiry reaches you. Some form services need you to confirm your inbox first. Check spam folders and follow any legitimate activation instructions. A visible “sent” message alone doesn’t replace checking that the inquiry arrived.

Connecting the address

The website is published to the agreed host and connected to your chosen address. Existing business email and domain settings need to be considered before changes. Some connection changes may take time to show consistently.

Your handoff

We’ll clarify which website files, accounts, access details, and instructions you receive. Your domain should stay under your control. Ask how you can request edits and whether the chosen setup lets you edit content yourself.

Keeping it current

Review hours, menu prices, services, links, and forms as your business changes. Keep domain renewals and necessary subscriptions active. New content, maintenance, and ongoing support are quoted separately unless your agreement includes them.

Your handy checklist

  • Approve the business details and final preview
  • Confirm a test inquiry arrives in your inbox
  • Keep a record of accounts, renewals, and support arrangements
Good things to ask

A few questions, answered.

Can I ask for updates after launch?

Yes. Send the link and the changes you need. We’ll check the work and agree on the price unless those updates are already included in your agreement.

Will the site automatically appear first in search?

No ranking is guaranteed. Clear content and a working site are a starting point; search visibility depends on many factors and may take time.

A real person, a little guidance

Still figuring it out? That’s okay.

I’m Luis. If you need help finding a domain or understanding something website-related, just mention it in your email. Advice is free; purchases and project work are discussed separately.
