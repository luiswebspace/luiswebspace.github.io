Free address or custom domain?

A platform address includes the host’s name, like luiswebspace.github.io. A custom domain uses your chosen name, such as yourbusiness.com. A platform address can help you get started, while a custom domain gives you an address you can use consistently across your marketing.

Choosing a name

Look for a short, readable name that customers can say and type easily. Check the spelling and whether it could be confused with another business. Availability changes, so a name isn’t yours until you register it successfully.

Buying and owning it

Register your domain in your own account with your own billing details. Keep your login safe and turn on the account’s security options. You should remain in control of the domain even if someone else builds your website.

Check the renewal, too

Before paying, review the registration price, renewal price, billing period, and any optional extras. A first-year promotion may cost more to renew. Keep your account email current and make a note of the renewal date.

Connecting it to the website

After the website and hosting are ready, the domain’s settings are connected to the host. If you already use business email on the domain, tell me before changes are made so those settings can be preserved. Connection updates may take time to appear everywhere.

Need a hand?

If you need help finding a domain or understanding a website question, mention it in your email. I’m happy to offer guidance free of charge. Domain purchases, hosting, subscriptions, and hands-on project work have their own costs and scope.

Your handy checklist

  • Write down a few names you like
  • Check first-year and renewal costs before purchasing
  • Keep the domain in an account you control
Good things to ask

A few questions, answered.

I already have a domain. Can I use it?

Usually, yes. Tell me the name and registrar. We’ll check the current setup and the requirements of your chosen host.

Does buying a domain include business email?

Not necessarily. An email inbox can be a separate service. Check what the registrar or email provider includes before choosing it.

Should I send you my password?

No. Don’t send passwords or payment details through email or the inquiry form. If access is needed, we’ll discuss an appropriate way to arrange it.
