A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

What kind of website can you make?

Business websites, service pages, portfolios, and landing pages are great starting points. If your idea needs booking, a store, or other special features, we’ll discuss what’s possible and include it in the scope.

Can you work from my drawing?

Yes. A sketch can help explain the layout and feel you have in mind. It doesn’t need to be polished. References and a description work too.

Do I have to buy a domain?

Not necessarily. Some hosting services offer a free address using their platform’s name. A custom address like yourbusiness.com usually has a registration fee and yearly renewal.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on your business, the number of pages, and the features your idea needs. We’ll agree on the price and included work before getting started.

Information / Before we build

From first message
to your own website.

Here’s how working with Webspace works, what affects the price, and who takes care of your domain.

01 — YOUR INQUIRY

Tell me about your business.

Send your business name, what you offer, the pages you need, and what visitors should be able to do. Include your budget, preferred timing, and any sketches or websites you like. An inquiry is a conversation, not a commitment to buy.

02 — YOUR ESTIMATE

Get a price for your idea.

I’ll review your needs and put together a project estimate. A simple one-page site generally involves less work than a multi-page site with booking, a shop, or custom features. The final fee depends on the agreed work—not just your type of business.

03 — THE AGREEMENT

Know what you’re paying for.

Before work starts, we agree in writing on the pages, features, design direction, included revisions, expected timing, and payment schedule. Any deposit and remaining balance will be stated in your quote. Extra requests may change the price and need your approval first.

04 — YOUR ADDRESS

Free address or custom domain?

A free address includes a hosting platform’s name, like this site’s github.io address. A custom domain, such as yourbusiness.com, is a separate purchase, subject to availability. You choose the name; I can help you check it and connect it.

Buy your domain in your own account. You stay in control of ownership, billing, and renewals. Don’t send passwords or card details through the inquiry form. If you already own a domain, tell me where it’s registered.

05 — THE COSTS

Your build fee isn’t every cost.

Your quote will separate my website design/build fee from any domain, hosting, or paid services. Domain prices vary by name and extension, and a first-year discount may renew at a higher price. Check the initial price, renewal price, and renewal date before paying.

Hosting, booking tools, store services, paid images, or other subscriptions may have separate recurring charges. Free hosting can suit a simple site; I’ll explain the options and any limits before we choose.

06 — DESIGN & FEEDBACK

Review it before it goes live.

You provide the business details, text, logo, and images you want used, or we agree on help preparing them. I build a preview, and you check the layout, wording, links, and business information. We make the revisions included in your quote.

07 — LAUNCH & AFTERCARE

Launch with a clear handoff.

Once you approve the site and the agreed payment requirements are met, we connect the chosen address and publish. We check mobile layouts, links, and contact forms. Some email forms need you to confirm your email before delivery works.

We’ll clarify which accounts and files you receive, how you can request changes, and whether ongoing support is included. Future edits and maintenance are quoted separately unless your agreement includes them. Keep domain renewals and any subscriptions active.

What should I send for an estimate?

A few details.

Your business, the goal of the site, approximate page count, must-have features, references, budget, and target date. Let me know whether you already have a domain, logo, text, and photos.
