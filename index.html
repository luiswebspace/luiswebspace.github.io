A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.

What kind of website can you make?

Business websites, service pages, portfolios, and landing pages are great starting points. If your idea needs booking, a store, or other special features, we’ll discuss what’s possible and include it in the scope.

Can you work from my drawing?

Yes. A sketch can help explain the layout and feel you have in mind. It doesn’t need to be polished. References and a description work too.

Do I have to buy a domain?

Not necessarily. Some hosting services offer a free address using their platform’s name. A custom address like yourbusiness.com usually has a registration fee and yearly renewal.

How much will my website cost?

It depends on your business, the number of pages, and the features your idea needs. We’ll agree on the price and included work before getting started.
