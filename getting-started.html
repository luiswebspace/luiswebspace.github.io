A friendly guide / Your first message

Tell me a little. We’ll figure out the rest.

You don’t need the perfect brief to get in touch. A few details about your business and what you’re hoping for are a lovely place to start.

✉

What to include

Start with your business name, what you offer, and who your customers are. Tell me whether you need your first website, a replacement, or a few changes to an existing one. If you have a current site, include the link.

Think about what visitors need

Should people read a menu, learn about a service, send an inquiry, or find your location? Pick the most important action first. We can discuss additional features once the basics are clear.

Bring whatever you have

A drawing, a phone note, a logo, photos, or a website you like can help explain the direction. Tell me what you like about a reference—its colors, layout, or atmosphere. Your website should still have its own identity.

Share a budget and a date

A rough budget helps me suggest a realistic scope. Mention any opening date or deadline so we can discuss availability and what needs to be ready first. Timing is agreed for your project.

What happens after you write

I’ll review your message and ask any questions needed to understand the work. Then we can discuss the direction and an estimate. Sending an inquiry doesn’t commit you to buying anything.

Your handy checklist

  • Your business name and current website, if you have one
  • The main thing you want your website to help people do
  • Your ideas, approximate budget, and preferred timing
Good things to ask

A few questions, answered.

I don’t have a logo or photos. Can I still ask?

Absolutely. Tell me what you already have and what you need help preparing. Any content preparation or paid assets can be discussed in the quote.

Can you work from a sketch?

Yes. A rough drawing is enough to start a conversation about layout. We’ll still need your business information and any content agreed for the site.

A real person, a little guidance

Still figuring it out? That’s okay.

I’m Luis. If you need help finding a domain or understanding something website-related, just mention it in your email. Advice is free; purchases and project work are discussed separately.
